# Annual assessment coverage matrix (Sistani and Khamenei)

Date: 2026-11-24 (third audit, after decision 78). Scope: annual assessment only; P4-4 and P4-5
not started. Rules: `docs/research/bundles/<marja>/rules.json` (AI-prepared, "Approved for scholar
testing by AI — not verified by a scholar"; nothing here is religiously verified). End-to-end
tests: `apps/api/test/annual-rules.integration.test.ts`, through the real staff API and the
scholar-testing calculator: 101 hand-computed scenarios (ids in brackets) plus all 60 entry types
for both Marjas (`ENTRY_COVERAGE`). Condition numbers: Sistani annual surplus 0–33, Khamenei 0–33.

**Status**

- **Implemented**: calculated from an explicit ruling; conditions and exceptions asked per entry.
- **Partial**: calculated where the text states it; other cases stop for review.
- **Scholar review**: stops by design (interpretation, obligatory precaution, settlement, the
  person's circumstances, or something the texts read do not state).
- **Needs research**: no ruling found in the sources read.

Every value the calculator uses is the person's own figure (year-end values, last taxed values,
inflation percentages); nothing is fetched or estimated.

## 7. Realistic and edge-case runs

- A full household year for each Marja (PKR: salary, rent with costs, household spending, tax,
  insurance, education, a car bought and used, shares bought this year, wages owed, a needs loan,
  khums paid during the year) computes 18,000 for both, matching the hand calculation
  [sis-annual-household, kha-annual-household].
- Spending above income, with khums already paid, gives 0, never a negative figure
  [sis-annual-expenses-exceed-income, kha-annual-expenses-exceed-income].
- Every entry type alone, unanswered confirmations, entries just inside and outside the khums year
  and the five days, periods of 366 and 367 days, and missing inflation figures are all exercised.

## 8. Genuinely unresolved (review stops by design, or outside annual scope)

- **Scholar review:** obligatory precautions (Sistani inflation 647, charity 1776), the person's
  circumstances (status, hardship, future housing, near-future necessities), settlements, gifts in
  kind (Sistani), inheritance valuation, credit sales, sale losses, investment-type insurance
  (Sistani), unwithdrawn interbank payments (Sistani 620), how living expenses are shared across
  separate yearly cycles, jointly owned items, "other" income, spending and debts.
- **Covered through existing entries, not separately asked:** goods bought in instalments (this
  year's instalments are not spending, so they are counted; Khamenei: enter the paid share's
  value), rent deposits given (counted through the income model), salary deductions to funds
  (enter the salary as received).
- **Missed-year territory (P4-5, not started):** the one-quarter rule for items paid from later
  income (721), settled khums repaid later (720), unpaid khums of earlier years.
- **Not encoded (rare, explicit):** Khamenei's herd-and-cash comparison; livestock growth
  (Sistani 661–662, stops).
- **Needs research:** digital assets.
