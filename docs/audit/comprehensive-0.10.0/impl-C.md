## Ids (Khamenei pack)

Questions added: `ftk-at-year-end`, `ftk-money-kind`, `ftk-money-source`, `ftk-bought-on-credit`,
`ftk-held-for-growth`, `ftk-former-necessity`, `ftk-savings-for-needs`, `ftk-amount-is-own-share`.
Removed: `ftk-funds`, `ftk-paid-or-exempt`, `holding-for-sale`. Reused (same meaning):
`holding-trade-or-growth`, `holding-inherited-unpaid`. Consultations added: `consult-ftk-year-end`,
`consult-ftk-unpaid-inflation`. Mapping rules added: `ftk-asset-review`, `ftk-joint`. No new
categories. Exports: `KHAMENEI_MONEY_KIND` (new), `KHAMENEI_FUNDS` (new values).

## Over-stops (for the scholar)

Necessity bought in an earlier year with that year's income (p.5 general exemption); proceeds of a
necessity sold after a year (Q137); borrowed money (Q35 part above unpaid instalments); digital
assets; `ftk-current` withheld under an inflation-only review (X1); older base withheld whenever any
other stop applies (incl. year-end with current entries present); earlier unpaid khums of a known
amount (KF-12).

## Limitations

- Invalid program answers (`ftk-year-known`, `ftk-at-year-end` non-boolean) are refused by the engine
  (test pins "no figure"); invalid per-entry answers ask again (QUESTION_ANSWER_INVALID).
- The asset tree is duplicated in JSON for `ftk-joint` (pack size); an engine "shared action" would
  avoid it.
- A credit purchase, partly-paid money and savings for near-future needs stop instead of computing
  the paid proportion / conditions (the text states them but they need inputs the sheet lacks).
