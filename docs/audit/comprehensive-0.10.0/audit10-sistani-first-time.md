## Over-stops where a text arguably settles the case

1. Necessity bought from in-year income and used only in a later year. 743 says «باید خمسش را بپردازد», and its example uses «قیمت فعلی».
2. Asset bought with income of the current, unfinished year. 736 implies nothing is due yet; current-year cash is already excluded, so the two are inconsistent but safe.
3. Specific-money purchase (737 ج): the inflation question is asked, but note 6 is attached only to the merchandise sentence.
4. Stolen merchandise (738 names it). Joint house share.
5. Legitimate mixtures of an ordinary pair with a year-old current-value item (LINK clauses 4 and 5).
6. Any debt, any income entry, KHUMS_PAID.
7. Price with `ft-price-item-owned`=no where the item was consumed. 738 would apply.

## What the linking fail-safe still misses

The fail-safe catches every one-sided case, any price-and-item kind mismatch involving `ft-assets-old`, and zero amounts. It cannot see counts or which item a price belongs to. Escapes:

| Case                                                                                                                                           | Needs                                  | Effect                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| N ordinary items, M < N price entries                                                                                                          | A false “yes” to `ft-price-entered`    | The price khums of N−M items is dropped (repro: 2 items, 1 price → 4,000)           |
| M > N price entries                                                                                                                            | A false “yes” to `ft-price-item-owned` | Extra prices are counted (repro: 7,000)                                             |
| A price claimed “ordinary” for an item actually in `ft-assets` (ended-year non-necessity, or 740 ب/741), when another ordinary pair is present | Contradictory answers                  | Double count (repro: 18,000 vs 12,000)                                              |
| **Current-value item plus its cost entered as a living or business expense (not ASSET_PURCHASE)**                                              | **No false answer; an honest path**    | **Double count (14,000 vs 10,000). This is AF-01, which can be fixed in the pack.** |

`ft-price-item` is never summed, so its mismatches cannot change a figure. All price-dropping and price-doubling cases except AF-01 depend on the person contradicting an explicit per-item question.
