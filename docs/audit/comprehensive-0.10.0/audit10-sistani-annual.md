# Audit 10: Sistani scholar-review pack 0.10.0, annual and missed-year (auditor AS)

Scope: the `annual-*` mapping rules (used by annual and missed-year), the shared annual program
(`annual` and `missedYear.perYear`, aggregate `khums-total` and `khums-remaining`), and the Sistani
questions, consultations and transcriptions they use. First-time is out of scope.

Method:

1. Own pass first. I walked every annual leaf, question and step in
   `scratchpad/sistani-inventory-0.10.0.md` and in `packages/marja-rules/src/review-packs/annual.ts`.
   I checked the quotes on official pages only:
   - English 4th ed. `/english/book/48/2306/`, `/2308/`, `/2310/`
   - Persian Tawdih `/persian/book/50/74/`
   - Jami' vol. 2 `/persian/book/26576/6160/` (598) and `/6383/` (684–685)
2. I ran about 130 combinations in one temporary test file
   (`packages/core/src/provisional/zz-audit10-sis-annual.tmp.test.ts`), through
   `prepareAssessmentInput` and then `calculate(…, {mode:'scholar-review'})`. The file is **deleted**.
3. Only then did I read `review/sistani-annual.md`, `decisions.md`, `impl-A1.md`, `impl-A2.md` and
   `docs/audit/*`.

Base facts unless stated: salary 42,000, household 30,000, and answers `living-within-status-all`,
`living-from-year-income` and `living-598-conditions` all yes. Baseline: COMPLETED, total 2,400.
Every figure here is a technical result of provisional transcriptions, not a religious answer.

Counts: **CRITICAL 0 · HIGH 1 · MEDIUM 3 · LOW 5.**

## Over-stops (a text arguably or plainly settles the case)

- A business trade loss → `consult-business-loss`. Jami' 684 offsets a loss of khums-paid working
  capital against «سود و درآمد همان سال».
- A capital loss larger than the business profit → `consult-capital-loss` (same 684 wording).
- A tool loss stopped by `capital-loss-paid`: 685 ب has no khums-paid condition (recorded).
- A car or house loan in the year of purchase → `consult-debts`. 1792 counts a house purchase as a
  living expense, and the first clause of 1803 may cover it.
- A personal-item loan after a year with profit left, or after a partial earlier deduction. Note 7
  allows the balance up to the cumulative surplus (recorded).
- SA-19: spending paid by a loan answered "not from this year's income" (recorded).
- An inherited holding answered "deceased left khums unpaid" where 1772's non-payer exception would
