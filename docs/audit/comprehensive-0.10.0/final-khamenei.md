# Final audit: Khamenei scholar-review pack 0.10.0 (annual, missed-year, first-time)

Auditor: independent, read-only. Pack: `sealedReviewPack('khamenei')`, metadata version **0.10.0**,
source `packages/marja-rules/src/review-packs/annual.ts`. Inventory walked:
`scratchpad/khamenei-inventory-0.10.0-final.md` (all questions, every leaf, consultations, the three
programs).

**Source text used.** The local text extraction of the office PDF «احکام خمس» (leader.ir book 215):
`<scratchpad>/txt/kha215.nfkc.txt`
(the NFKC copy of `kha215.txt`). leader.ir was not fetched; no third-party site was used. Page
numbers are the extraction's; questions 1–222 use index numbers, 223+ the printed Persian numbers.

**Runs.** One temporary file `packages/core/src/provisional/zz-final-kha.tmp.test.ts`
(`prepareAssessmentInput` → `calculate(…, {mode:'scholar-review'})`), run with
`pnpm --filter @khums/core exec vitest run …` (4/4 passed, 178 s), then **deleted**. No other
repository file was touched; no commit; API, dev servers and Playwright not started. Raw results
(synthetic values only): `scratchpad/review/final-kha-runs.json`.

- **Annual and missed-year, exhaustive:** a DFS over every fact-mapping rule of the sealed pack, for
  every workflow, kind and subtype it matches, every leaf plus an unanswered and an invalid answer
  at every question: **1,279 runs**. Each leaf ran inside a baseline sheet (annual: salary 40,000,
  business 30,000, household 20,000 → 10,000; missed: 2023 salary 30,000 / household 20,000 and
  2024 salary 50,000 / business 30,000 / household 20,000), with a 10,000 test entry. The total's
  change was checked against the leaf's terminal (+2,000 counted, −2,000 deducted, unchanged
  excluded, withheld for review categories, refused or clarified for mapping stops). **0
  mismatches.** Stops per terminal are as designed (for example `unpaid-khums` → only
  `earlier-unpaid` withheld; `khums-paid` → `khums-remaining` (annual) or the year (missed) withheld).
- **First-time, exhaustive:** every leaf of every first-time rule, all subtypes, unanswered and
  invalid; every categorised leaf under four program-answer sets (year known and at year-end; not at
  year-end; at-year-end unanswered; year unknown): **3,685 runs**. 0 inconsistencies: the total, both
  halves and `ftk-current` are withheld whenever any review, settlement, debt, unknown year,
  not-at-year-end or inflation stop exists; `ftk-older-base` appears only behind the inflation stop.
- **Targeted scenarios (55):** every AK/AT reproduction, multi-entry sheets (annual: 14 entries of 12
  kinds → 5,400, hand-checked; missed: years with different entries → 2,400 + 5,000 = 7,400
  PARTIAL), years with different answers, an unanswered year (refused, INPUT_MISSING_REQUIRED_ANSWER),
  old answer ids and values (refused or clarification), UNKNOWN (clarification), ESTIMATED and RANGE
  (`consult-entry`), and missed-year unknown amounts (clarification or `consult-missed-settlement`).
  Arithmetic was exact in every calculated case.

## 4. Over-stops (a text arguably settles the case; not defects)

  withholdings.

  picked from the shore (p.24 «خمس ندارد»).

- `living-paid-after-first-income` "no" for gift, inheritance or khums-paid money spent after the
  first income (p.40 allows the deduction).

- Freelance and rental costs are never deducted (always `consult-work-costs`, or ignored behind a
  separate-account loss).

  only «سپرده مخمّس»).
- First-time: a necessity from an inseparable mix (Q252 might give a settlement); survivor's pension,
  stipend, bride-price → review; loans (Q35); the proceeds of selling a necessity (Q137).
- Missed years: any unknown amount stops the whole sheet (`consult-missed-settlement`, KA-07
  decision).
- Previously recorded over-stops (Q223, Q188/Q11, Q189, KA-01, KA-13, X2, KA-20) are unchanged.

## 5. Limitations

- Source: only the local extraction of the office PDF. The extraction has OCR artefacts in places
  (for example «میت واند»), but every quote used above was read in context. leader.ir was not
  re-fetched (it may 404).
- Only the pack and the core and engine pipeline were exercised. The web UI, Urdu content, the e2e
  journeys, the API and stale-answer handling (AF-05 analogue) were not run.
- The exhaustive runs check behaviour against each leaf's terminal and the program. Whether a
  terminal is religiously right was judged by reading, for every leaf group, against the excerpts.
  For leaves that end in a stop this is fail-safe by construction.
- Rounding stays at full precision (maintainer decision); not re-examined.
- FK-02 involves an internal tension in the text (Q187 has no condition; Q197/Q198 make it depend on
  entitlement). The fix is a stop, not a choice between the readings.
