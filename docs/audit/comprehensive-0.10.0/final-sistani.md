# Final verification audit: Sistani scholar-review pack 0.10.0 (annual, missed-year, first-time), auditor FS

This audit is read-only and independent. Every figure below is a TECHNICAL result of provisional
transcriptions, not a religious answer. Every rule is still `UNVERIFIED`.

## Method

1. **Own pass first.** I walked every leaf, question, consultation and program step in
   `scratchpad/sistani-inventory-0.10.0-final.md`, against `packages/marja-rules/src/review-packs/annual.ts`.
   I checked the excerpts against official pages only. Raw copies are in `scratchpad/fs-src/`:
   - Tawdih `/persian/book/50/74/` (1769–1850)
   - Jami' vol. 2 `/persian/book/26576/6550/`, `6551` (737), `6552` (738), `6553` (739–744) and `6184` (626)

   The quoted excerpts match the page text. That includes 1771 («اگر از مخارج سالش زیاد بیاید»),
   1772, 1776, 1783, 1799, 1801, 1803, 1816–1817, 1825, 1827, 1836–1837, 737 with notes 1–6, 738,
   and 739–744.

2. **One temporary test.** `packages/core/src/provisional/zz-final-sis.tmp.test.ts` ran
   `prepareAssessmentInput` and then `calculate(…, {mode:'scholar-review'})`.
   - An automatic walker over the sealed pack's own fact mapping ran **every leaf of every rule ×
     every subtype × every workflow**, plus an invalid and an unanswered branch at every question.
     That is 2,330 runs, with outputs in `scratchpad/fs-src/leaves.json` and `leaves.txt`.
   - 70 combination sheets ran on top of that (`scratchpad/fs-src/combos.json`).
   - **The file is deleted.**
3. Only after that did I read the AS/AF audit, impl-D/A1/B and decisions.md, and reproduce each item.

**Mechanical results: clean.** These invariants hold in all 2,330 leaf runs:

- No half, amount still to pay, or first-time part appears when the total is withheld.
- A missed-year aggregate is COMPLETE only when every year is COMPLETED.
- An unanswered or invalid entry question gives `NEEDS_CLARIFICATION`.
- Estimated and range amounts give `consult-entry`. An unknown amount gives clarification. A date outside the period is refused.
- Every COMPLETED total equals the expected arithmetic:
  - annual base 2,400; +10,000 profit → 4,400; +10,000 living → 400
  - first-time: one-fifth of the counted categories

The big annual sheet has 16 entries of mixed kinds and gives **3,600 / still to pay 2,600 / earlier
unpaid 900**. I checked it by hand: (42,000 + 15,000 + 8,000 + 5,000 + 3,000 − 3,000) − 36,000 − 16,000 = 18,000.

## Over-stops (a text arguably or plainly settles the case; record for the scholar)

- Living expenses paid from capital before the first profit of the year → `consult-living-source`.
  1800 «از سرمایه خرج کند، و پیش از تمام شدن سال منفعتی به دستش آید، می‌تواند … از منافع کسر کند».
- Repaying a living loan of a year with no profit from later income → `consult-debts`. 1803 «در
  صورت اوّل می‌تواند قرض خود را از درامد سالهای بعد ادا کند، و به آن مقدار خمس تعلق نمی‏گیرد».
- A capital loss larger than the business profit → `consult-capital-loss`. Persian 1801 deducts it
  «از منافع همان سال» (the year's profit, not only the business profit).
- The remainder of mined product or diving gems for a person **whose work is diving or mining** →
  `consult-special-remainder`. 1843 «اگر خمس آنها را بدهد و اموال مخمّس مذکور، از مخارج سالش زیاد
  بیاید، لازم نیست دوباره خمس آن را بدهد».
- Unknown-source merchandise that has not risen → settlement. 741 gives the current value, which
  then equals the price.
- Earlier recorded over-stops still apply: SA-19, a car or house loan in its purchase year, a
  co-owned house (AS-3), a gift in kind left over (AS-4), unknownOther (744), a necessity not used in
  the purchase year (743), and LINK clauses 4 and 5.
- If adopted, FS-01, FS-03 and FS-04 add over-stops of their own (ordinary purchase, net pay entered, no actual devaluation).
