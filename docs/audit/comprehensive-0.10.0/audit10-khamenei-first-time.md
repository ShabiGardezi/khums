# Audit 10: Khamenei scholar-review pack 0.10.0, first-time workflow

Read-only audit. Pack checked: `sealedReviewPack('khamenei')`, metadata version 0.10.0. Source code:
`packages/marja-rules/src/review-packs/annual.ts` (`khameneiFirstTimeParts`, `KHAMENEI_MONEY_KIND`,
`KHAMENEI_FUNDS`).

**Source text used.** The local text extraction of the office PDF «احکام خمس» (leader.ir book 215),
`<scratchpad>/txt/kha215.txt`,
NFKC-normalised into a scratch copy so it could be searched. I did not fetch leader.ir or any
third-party site. Question numbers follow the PDF: 1–222 are the index numbers, 223–299 are
printed.

**Method.**

1. Own pass first: I walked the inventory and the source against the excerpts before reading
   any review notes.
2. Temp test `packages/core/src/provisional/zz-audit10-kha-ft.tmp.test.ts` (now deleted). It ran
   `prepareAssessmentInput` and then `calculate(…, {mode:'scholar-review'})`.
   - It walked every leaf of every first-time mapping rule by DFS over the sealed pack's action
     tree: every choice of every question, plus unanswered and invalid answers.
   - It covered every subtype of each rule: 11 asset subtypes on `ftk-asset`, `JOINT_OWNERSHIP`,
     `BUSINESS_STOCK`, `RECEIVABLE`/`DIGITAL_ASSET`, 4 balance subtypes, `MIXED_FUNDS`, all
     income, expense and liability subtypes.
   - Every leaf that ends in a category was rerun under four program-answer sets: year known and
     at year-end; year known, not at year-end; year known, at-year-end unanswered; year unknown.
   - In total 3,301 leaf runs and 32 multi-entry or edge scenarios.
3. Then I read `review/khamenei-first-time.md` (KF-01…KF-24), `review/decisions.md`,
   `review/impl-C.md` and `docs/audit/*`.

**Integrity results (all as designed).**

- Behaviour is identical across subtypes within each rule (0 inconsistencies).
- The behaviour matches the 0.10.0 inventory leaf for leaf.
- Unanswered or invalid per-entry answers give a clarification with no figure.
- An estimated or range amount gives `consult-entry` for the whole sheet. An unknown amount is
  asked for. A negative amount is `UNSUPPORTED`.
- An invalid program answer is refused by the engine. Missing program answers give
  `missing-answer`.
- A pending entry (review, debts, receivable, stock, mixed, income, expense) withholds the total,
  `ftk-current`, `ftk-older-base` and both halves. So do settlement, an unknown year and
  "not at year-end".
- `ftk-older-base` is visible only under an inflation-only review. It is labelled "before
  inflation (not the amount due)".

Severity counts: **CRITICAL 0 · HIGH 1 · MEDIUM 2 · LOW 4**
