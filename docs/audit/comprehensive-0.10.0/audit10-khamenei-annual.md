`packages/core/src/provisional/zz-audit10-kha-annual.tmp.test.ts`, was created, run with
`pnpm --filter @khums/core exec vitest run …` (`prepareAssessmentInput` → `calculate(…, {mode:'scholar-review'})`
on `sealedReviewPack('khamenei')`, 0.10.0), and **deleted** at the end. The other auditors' temp
files (`zz-audit10-kha-ft…`, `zz-audit10-sis-annual…`) were left alone. The raw run log
(synthetic values only) is at
`<scratchpad>/review/audit10-kha-annual-runs.txt`
(70 combinations: A1–A15, B1–B9, C1–C5, D1–D11, E1–E13, F1–F7, M1–M16, X1–X21).

**Source used.** I checked quotes against the local text extraction of the office PDF
(`…/7fdaaf89…/scratchpad/txt/kha215.txt`, the «احکام خمس» leader.ir book 215). I normalised it with
NFKC into my scratchpad so it could be searched. I did not fetch leader.ir and used no third-party
site. PDF page numbers are those of the extraction.

Every figure below is a technical result under the provisional transcriptions, never a religious
answer. Every proposed fix is a consult stop, a label change, or a question whose distinction the
quoted excerpt states. No ruling, rate, threshold or exemption is proposed.

Severity (same scale as the KA review): CRITICAL = wrong figure in a plainly settled case;
HIGH = a final figure (total, halves, remaining, khums-year, known-years) shown where the text
states a condition that was never asked, or that is misleading against the text; MEDIUM = a
figure that depends on an ambiguous or double-countable entry, or a misleading part or label;
LOW = wording, consistency, or a low-likelihood entry issue.

**Counts: CRITICAL 0 · HIGH 3 · MEDIUM 5 · LOW 5** (13 items).

Baseline fixture: annual period 2025-07-01 to 2026-06-30, USD. Salary 40,000 and household
20,000 with the 0.10.0 defaults (`living-within-status`, `living-from-year-income`,
`period-is-one-khums-year` = yes; `living-leftover`, `business-costs-lasting` = no) give
total 4,000. Missed years: 2023 (salary 30,000, household 20,000) and 2024 (salary 50,000,
household 20,000). "Stops" leaves out the permanent `consult-CTQ` and `consult-payee`.
