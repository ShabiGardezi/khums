## Legend

**Status:**

- `CANDIDATE`: an official heading names the topic; the passage has not been read.
- `SEARCH`: no heading names it; search the Khums chapter, then the istifta' (Q&A), before any
  `SOURCE_SILENT` finding (the finding template needs the sections searched).
- `ATTRIBUTION`: a candidate exists, but its author is unestablished (all Khamenei items, see
  PRIMARY_SOURCE_INVENTORY §3.3–§3.5). Khamenei pointers are headings and the site's question
  numbers only (updated 2026-11-22 from leader.ir).

**Locator label** (pass 2, 2026-11-22; PRIMARY_SOURCE_INVENTORY §1): a tool-assisted check that the
cited number or heading exists where stated, per source code. `VERIFIED`: edition stated and
numbering agrees across counterparts. `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`: located, but edition, counterpart or
attribution not fully established. `UNVERIFIED`: heading-level only or not located. It is **not** the
scholar's verification and says nothing about what the text rules. Evidence: `M`, `EN` →
PRIMARY_SOURCE_INVENTORY §2.1; `J` → edition stated, masʾalah numbers not extracted; `K1` → text
attributed and archive-dated before 2026-02-28 (§3.3), not scholar-verified; `K2` → numbers located, no author or date shown (class C); `K4` Q224–Q271 →
number and heading match the Persian (§3.2.1); other `K4` numbers → English index only.

**Verified reference:** filled only after the scholar verifies the exact passage. All are empty today.
