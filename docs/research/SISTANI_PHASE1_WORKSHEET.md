   English follows), the _Tawdih Jami'_, or the Arabic _Minhaj_?

   created 2025-12-07) carries your endorsement and seal on p. 3 and the title «با تجدید نظر و

   that the English follows, especially in the starred rulings? (PRIMARY_SOURCE_INVENTORY §2.1.)
3. ~~What does the `*` mean?~~ **Resolved 2026-11-22:** Appendix 3 of the English 4th edition says
   `*` marks rulings revised since the previous English edition. Five rulings in this range are
   starred (1772, 1782, 1798, 1803, 1810); the earlier `*` on 1814 was an extraction error. The
   Persian page marks none. Which Persian edition introduced each revision is not stated.

## 4. Workflow once the scholar's account exists

1. **Source:** record _Tawdih al-Masa'il_ (Persian) and _Islamic Laws_ (English) as sources, with
   the edition details the scholar establishes; verify them.
2. **Passages:** one per masʾalah, verbatim from the canonical original. Locator: page `/50/74/`,
   masʾalah number, paragraph if needed. Verify.
3. **Translations:** the office's English text as an English translation of the passage, with the
   translator as the office states it; Urdu where verified. Review.
4. **Findings:** one per question (e.g. `INC-01`), citing the verified passages. Conditions,
   exceptions and precaution levels are recorded exactly as the text states them. Verify, then approve.
5. **Rulings:** from approved findings. Approve when ready.
6. **Encoding (engineering):** a staff-only scholar-review pack that encodes **exactly** the approved
   rulings, citing each as `<ruling id>@<version>`, with in-pack provenance. Every case the text leaves
   open becomes a consult stop, never a default. It is tested with decimal-safe amounts and labelled
   **PROVISIONAL — FOR SCHOLAR REVIEW ONLY**.
