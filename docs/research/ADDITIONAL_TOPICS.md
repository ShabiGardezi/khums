## 1. Matrix questions outside the 70 that the sources answer

| Question                                     | What was found (both Marjas unless noted)                                                                                                                               |

| ADJ-01 Gold and silver                       | Gold coins and bullion bought from income are liable at year-end value; jewellery worn within status is mu'nah                                                          |
| ADJ-02 Mixed lawful/unlawful wealth          | Paying a fifth of the whole makes it lawful when owner and amount are unknown; other cases are charity or settlement, not khums                                         |
| ADJ-03 Mahr                                  | Mahr received is not liable; unpaid mahr is deductible in the year of marriage (Sistani Q&A 27, Jami' 704)                                                              |
| ADJ-04 Distribution                          | Two equal halves; Sistani: the sayyids' half may be given directly, the Imam's half to the most learned or his agent; Khamenei: both halves through his office or agent |

| SUR-09 Khums paid during or after the year   | Paying a past fifth from a later year's income requires paying a quarter, or a fifth of the payment too (Sistani Jami' 721; Khamenei Q246–247)                          |

## 2. Topics found that no matrix question covers

1. **Who counts as having an occupation**, and several yearly cycles (Sistani Jami' 563–576; Khamenei Q228, Q245).
2. **The five-day window after the year end** for spending on needs and repaying debts (Khamenei).
3. **Inflation**: Khamenei deducts it by fatwa (price rises, capital, and unpaid past khums paid "with inflation"); Sistani treats an inflation-only rise by obligatory precaution (Jami' 647), so a follower may refer to the next most learned (Tawdih 6).
4. **Debts after the year's first income** (Khamenei) vs **the deduction rule with lasting and one-off needs** (Sistani Jami' 703–711).
5. **Capital hardship exception**: Sistani requires paying what can be paid even by instalments (Jami' 640); Khamenei exempts even if instalments are possible or sufficiency is doubtful.
6. **Pension and savings-fund deductions**: ownership by the employer vs a loan repaid after retirement (Sistani Q&A 4); compulsory vs agreed deductions (Khamenei).
7. **Receivables**: pay now on present value or later when collected (Sistani Jami' 715).
8. **Instalment purchases**: liable in proportion to instalments paid (both).
9. **Death during the year, minors and the insane** (outside the calculator: one living adult).
10. **Iran-specific instruments** (Shetab transfers, justice shares, veterans' payments): recorded, stopped for review elsewhere.

## 3. Gaps (not found in the sources read)

- **INV-05 digital assets**: no ruling in Tawdih, Tawdih Jami' vol. 2 or the Sistani Khums Q&A pages (Persian, English, Arabic), nor in «احکام خمس» or «اجوبة الاستفتائات». A `SOURCE_SILENT` finding needs the scholar.
- **INV-04 investment losses (Sistani)**: losses are stated for business capital and trading (Jami' 684–688), not for investments held for income.
- **CTQ-06, CTQ-07 (Sistani)**: concern a deceased Marja; not applicable while he is living.
- **CTQ-07 (Khamenei)**: which office answers new questions for his followers after his death is not stated in his own texts.
