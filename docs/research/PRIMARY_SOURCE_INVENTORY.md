### 2.1 Edition and numbering check (pass 2, 2026-11-22)

**English _Islamic Laws_, 4th edition: facts from its own front matter**

- It is The World Federation of KSIMC's translation, published on sistani.org. The foreword says the
  first edition (1994) was published "in accordance with the wishes of His Eminence". The
  translator's preface to the 3rd edition thanks the offices in Qum and London for clarifications;
  the 4th-edition preface thanks a member of the Qum office for answering questions. **The office
  is not named as publisher.**
- The translation adds annotations, footnotes and glosses in square brackets, many based on
  _Minhāj al-Ṣāliḥīn_ (3rd-edition preface, convention 2; 4th-edition preface). **These are the
  translator's, not the ruling text**, and must be kept out of passages.
- Revised rulings are marked `[*]` and listed in Appendix 3 `/8245/`. The foreword and preface name
  six revisions as most significant: 597, 1498, 1562, 1694, 1699 and **1803**.
- The preface mentions a World Federation booklet, _Khums: A Brief Guide_. It is **not** an office
  publication (Tier 4; §4).

**Discrepancies (resolved or flagged)**

1. **Resolved:** the `*` on 1814 in the earlier worksheet was an extraction error. The English page
   stars 1772, 1782, 1798, 1803 and 1810 only, exactly matching Appendix 3.
2. **Resolved (pass 3, PDF):** in the PDF's Appendix 3 (p. 532) only **597, 1498, 1562, 1694, 1699
   and 1803** are set in bold (font `TimesNewRomanPS-BoldMT`): the six named in the foreword. The
   list of numbers matches the web page. The web bolding (1772, 1810 and others) is an HTML
   conversion artefact.
3. **Resolved (pass 3, PDF):** the PDF has section "7. LAND THAT A DHIMMĪ PURCHASES FROM A MUSLIM" and
   Ruling 1850 (p. 311). They are missing only from the web pages. The PDF's Khums chapter (pp.
   300–313) has the same seven sections as the Persian, and stars the same rulings as the web (1772,
   1782, 1798, 1803, 1810, 1861, 1865).
4. **Flagged, narrowed (pass 3, PDF):** the Persian PDF (`persian-tozih-edition1447.pdf`, 607 pages,
   created 2025-12-07) has a title page «توضیح المسائل — با تجدید نظر و اضافات و اصلاحات — مطابق فتاوی
   حضرت آیة الله العظمی آقای حاج سید علی حسینی سیستانی» and, on p. 3, a handwritten endorsement with
   seal: «عمل به این رساله شریفه مجزی و مبرئ ذمه است إن شاء الله تعالی — علی الحسینی السیستانی». It prints
   **no edition number, date or colophon**. Khums masāʾil 1768–1866 are on PDF pp. 329–345 (1850 on
   p. 343). Whether it is the 36th edition (2021) or a later one is **not established**: the file
   name and creation date point to a 1447 AH (2025) text. **Same numbers may hide different text**,
   especially for the starred rulings.
5. **Resolved (pass 3):** the Urdu page runs to 1870 only because it also holds the next chapter
   (enjoining good, 1867–1870). Its Khums numbering is the same as the Persian.
6. **Flagged:** the English chapter page 2312 cites "Ruling 1805" and "Ruling 1851" inside other
   rulings (cross-references, not new numbers).
