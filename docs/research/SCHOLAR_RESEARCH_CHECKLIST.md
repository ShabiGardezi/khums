# Scholar Research Checklist (M5.4)

> For the authorized scholar of `sistani` or `khamenei` (and anyone researching under their
> authority). One page per step; tick every box before moving on. The full rules are in
> [REVIEW_PROCESS.md](REVIEW_PROCESS.md) and [RESEARCH_PLAN.md](RESEARCH_PLAN.md); this list does
> not replace them. Starting points: [PRIMARY_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md](PRIMARY_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md) and
> [RESEARCH_PREPARATION_MATRIX.md](RESEARCH_PREPARATION_MATRIX.md). Everything is recorded in the
> **Research workspace** (`/admin/research`), never in these files.

## 0. Before you start

- [ ] You have your own staff account with the `GOVERNING_SCHOLAR` role for your Marja (an
      administrator grants it; the SUPER_ADMIN account cannot hold it).
- [ ] You know the scope: the 70 required questions only (RESEARCH_MATRIX §2), one Marja at a time.
      Nothing is copied from one Marja to the other.
- [ ] For `khamenei`: read RESEARCH_MATRIX §9 and start with `CTQ-01…08`.

## 1. Source (workspace: Sources)

- [ ] It is published by the Marja's own office (Tier 1 risalah or Tier 2 istifta'), not a
      secondary site, booklet, forum or AI tool (SOURCE_INVENTORY §1.1).
- [ ] Recorded as printed: original title, original language, publisher, edition and publication
      date (original calendar and Gregorian), exact URL.
- [ ] Authorship and authenticity checked and stated, including how you established them.
- [ ] Access status and copyright recorded; if copyright is not stated, ask the office before
      archiving a full copy.
- [ ] A [source-verification record](templates/source-verification.template.md) is complete.
      For `khamenei`, it also covers original authorship, edition, and whether the source was
      published before or after the recorded date of death. Nothing from the current office is
      attributed to him without proof.
- [ ] Verify the source in the workspace only after all of the above.

## 2. Passage (workspace: Passages)

- [ ] Copied verbatim from the canonical original-language text (Arabic or Persian), with no
      paraphrase and no "cleaning up". Translations are never entered as the original.
- [ ] Exact locator: volume, page, mas'alah or istifta' number, section, paragraph and URL, as
      available.
- [ ] Re-located in the archived copy; any discrepancy noted.
- [ ] The words stating fatwa or precaution are recorded as printed.
- [ ] Excerpt only, unless copyright is `PERMITTED`.
- [ ] Verify the passage, which cites the verified source revision.

## 3. Translation (workspace: Translations)

- [ ] English or Urdu, linked to the exact verified passage revision.
- [ ] Translator named. Machine-drafted text is declared as AI involvement and reviewed word by word.
- [ ] Marked "reviewed" only after you compared it with the original. The original always governs.

## 4. Finding (workspace: Findings)

- [ ] One catalogue question; the question id is chosen from the list (never a new question).
- [ ] Cites at least one **verified** passage revision (D14), plus only reviewed translations of
      those passages. Never a source directly.
- [ ] Conclusion and explanation state only what the cited text says; conditions, exceptions,
      qualifications and precaution level are recorded as stated.
- [ ] Ambiguities go to "unresolved questions" and, where needed, to the consultation register
      (RESEARCH_MATRIX §6). Nothing is guessed or "completed".
- [ ] If nothing is found, record the sections searched before any `SOURCE_SILENT` conclusion.
- [ ] Verify, then approve, only when you are satisfied.

## 5. Ruling (workspace: Rulings)

- [ ] Built from **approved** findings of the same Marja, citing the verified passages it relies on.
- [ ] Conditions, exceptions and precaution level carried over as the findings state them.
- [ ] Approve only after review; a correction is a new version (the old one stays in history).
      Withdraw with a reason if needed.
- [ ] No ruling is assumed to be shared with the other Marja.

## 6. Provisional rule pack and review (staff only)

- [ ] Rule packs are authored content that cite rulings `<id>@<version>`. Calculation logic is
      never generated from a finding or ruling.
- [ ] Provisional calculations stay staff-only, labelled **PROVISIONAL — FOR SCHOLAR REVIEW ONLY**.
- [ ] Review the calculator's behaviour against the original fatwas. Report issues through
      feedback; corrections go through new ruling or pack versions.
- [ ] Each rule shows a review status (Sistani and Khamenei review packs, M5.4): only your
      approved rulings, encoded in an approved pack version, make a rule **Scholar approved**.
      Rules you have not researched stay **Partially verified** or **Source unverified**, and every
      calculation that depends on them stops for review. Request a correction with feedback
      (category "Rule content"); withdraw a ruling in the workspace to reject it.
- [ ] Rules marked **PROVISIONAL TRANSCRIPTION** were encoded by AI from the published text
      (CLAUDE.md rule 1): check each excerpt against the original and the calculation it drives, and
      request a correction or record your own findings and rulings to replace it.
- [ ] Test scenarios show the technically expected behaviour only. Record an expected religious
      result only through the governed workflow; never accept a generated one.

## 7. What you need to verify first (from the preparation files)

1. Which text is canonical for Sistani's Khums rulings: the Arabic _Minhaj al-Salihin_, the
   Persian _Tawdih al-Masa'il_ or the _Tawdih Jami'_ (PRIMARY_SOURCE_INVENTORY §5.1), and which
   Persian edition is on the site compared with the 36th edition the English follows (§2.1).
2. Edition details and exact page or ruling numbers for every item marked "not shown" or "not
   checked".
3. For PS-KHA-1, its print edition and date and the office-approval statement (archive evidence in
   PRIMARY_SOURCE_INVENTORY §3.3). Authorship, edition and date of every other Khamenei item on leader.ir (PS-KHA-1…8; open questions in PRIMARY_SOURCE_INVENTORY §3.4), and access to
   khamenei.ir or its dated archives.
4. Copyright and archiving permission from each office.
5. The four Sistani and 3 Khamenei questions marked `SEARCH`, and every `CANDIDATE` pointer: a
   heading is only a place to look, not an answer.
